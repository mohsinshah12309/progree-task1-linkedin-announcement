## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions to Publish
1. Open the preview via `index.html` or copy the text from `caption.txt`.
2. Navigate to [LinkedIn Feed](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/).
3. Click **"Start a post"**.
4. Paste the caption text. Tag `@Progree` so it links directly to the official Progree company page.
5. Click **"Add media"** and upload your high-resolution official Internship Offer Letter.
6. Verify hashtags (`#fullstack #webdev #javascript #progree`) and click **Post**.
